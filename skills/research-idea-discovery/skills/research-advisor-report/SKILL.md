# Research Advisor Report

The final output of research discovery must help a researcher make decisions, not merely show audit logs.

## Required report

1. Executive conclusion
- status: STRONG CANDIDATE / PROMISING SEED / NEEDS EVIDENCE / HIGH RISK / REJECT
- action: KEEP / REFINE / PIVOT / STOP / RESEARCH MORE

2. Plain-language idea explanation
Explain the problem, insight, and why it matters without jargon.

3. Motivation
Separate practical motivation, scientific motivation, existing assumptions, and refined research question.

4. Related work positioning
Show what previous methods solve and what remains open.

5. Scientific hypothesis
State a falsifiable hypothesis. Do not confuse hypothesis with contribution.

6. Potential contributions
Only after evidence review. Do not force three contributions if the evidence supports fewer.

7. Scientific story
Observation -> Failure -> Root cause -> Insight -> Method -> Evidence.

8. Reviewer attack
List strongest rejection arguments and required evidence.

9. Feasibility
Datasets, baselines, resources, and unknowns.

10. Decisive next experiment
Find the cheapest experiment that can kill or support the idea.

11. Final mentor summary
Explain in one sentence what the research is really about.

All claims must be marked VERIFIED, CONDITIONAL, UNKNOWN, or HYPOTHESIS.
